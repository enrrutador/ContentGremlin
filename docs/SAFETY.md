# ContentGremlin – Safety & Anti-Demonetization Rules

These rules are **hard-coded** and cannot be disabled. They exist to protect the user’s channel.

## Core Rules

1. **Never copy content literally**
   - No full scripts, no near-identical titles, no reused thumbnails from the reference channel.

2. **Pattern extraction only**
   - The analyzer may look at topics, structure, average duration, hook style, title patterns and audience signals.
   - It must never download or store the actual video content for regeneration.

3. **Originality enforcement**
   - Every idea and script passes through originality checks.
   - If an idea is too close to existing content, it is discarded or heavily rewritten.

4. **Upload protection**
   - Uploading is only possible when:
     - The user is in Autonomous mode **and** has previously given consent, or
     - The user explicitly approves the upload in Supervised mode.

5. **No deceptive practices**
   - No fake engagement, no misleading metadata, no impersonation of other creators.

## Why these rules exist

YouTube actively fights reused and derivative content that provides no new value.  
ContentGremlin is designed to help creators produce **original** work inspired by successful patterns — not to create spam or copies.

The goal is sustainable growth without risking strikes or demonetization.
